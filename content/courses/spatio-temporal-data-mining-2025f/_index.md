## Course Information

- **Course Name**: Spatio-Temporal Data Mining (时空数据挖掘)
- **Semester**: Fall 2025
- **Instructor**: Assoc. Prof. Songyu Ke (柯嵩宇)
- **Department**: College of Computer and Data Science, Fuzhou University
- **Audience**: Graduate students in Computer Science and Technology

## Course Description

This course systematically introduces the key concepts, methods, and applications of spatio-temporal data mining. Students will learn how to manage, model, and mine spatio-temporal data in urban computing scenarios, and understand how modern AI techniques are applied to spatio-temporal problems.

Main topics include:
- Overview of spatio-temporal data mining and urban computing
- Traditional machine learning methods for time series and spatial data
- Spatio-temporal data management and processing
- AI for spatio-temporal data (AI4ST)
- Capturing spatio-temporal correlations (spatial/temporal components, transformers)
- Advanced topics (spatio-temporal large models, etc.)
