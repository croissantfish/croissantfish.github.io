abstract: "Spatio-temporal graph prediction is an essential task in urban computing, with applications in traffic forecasting, air quality prediction, and crowd flow estimation. While many hand-crafted neural architectures have been proposed, they require substantial domain expertise and may be suboptimal for specific datasets. In this paper, we propose AutoSTG+, an automatic framework to discover the optimal network for spatio-temporal graph prediction. AutoSTG+ extends the AutoSTG framework by introducing multiple meta-graphs and a vector-quantized dynamic graph generator to better capture spatial and temporal correlations. Specifically, we construct meta-graphs from spatial, temporal, and feature similarities, and enhance the graph meta-knowledge learning process to extract richer meta-knowledge from multiple meta-graphs. Furthermore, we propose a partial dynamic graph module called Vector-Quantized Dynamic Graph (VQDG) to learn representations from dynamic temporal information and generate the corresponding dynamic meta-graph. Extensive experiments on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that AutoSTG+ can discover effective architectures and achieve superior performance compared to both manually designed models and its predecessor AutoSTG."
