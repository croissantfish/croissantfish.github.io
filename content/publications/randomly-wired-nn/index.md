abstract: "Traffic forecasting is a fundamental task in intelligent transportation systems, aiming to predict future traffic conditions based on historical observations. While deep learning models, especially graph neural networks, have achieved remarkable progress in traffic forecasting, their architectures are often manually designed, which requires substantial expertise and may be suboptimal. Randomly wired neural networks have shown promising results in computer vision by exploring unconventional network topologies. In this paper, we extend this idea to spatio-temporal data and propose efficient spatio-temporal randomly wired neural networks for traffic forecasting. Our approach leverages random graph generation to construct diverse network topologies and employs neural architecture search to identify the most effective ones. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate that our method achieves competitive performance with significantly reduced computational costs compared to manually designed counterparts."
