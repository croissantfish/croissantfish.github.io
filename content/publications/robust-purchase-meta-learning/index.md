abstract: "Purchase prediction is critical for various applications, such as targeted marketing and merchandise assortment planning. While traditional prediction methods achieve promising results, they heavily rely on the accumulation of historical data, which often suffers from data scarcity and cold-start problems. To address these challenges, we propose a deep meta learning framework, which is able to rapidly adapt to a new purchase prediction task with only a few samples. Specifically, we design a novel spatio-temporal multi-task purchase prediction network that jointly predicts multiple purchase patterns and effectively captures the spatio-temporal correlations. Furthermore, we introduce a meta learning strategy to learn the initial parameters of the network from various related tasks, enabling fast adaptation to new tasks with limited training data. Extensive experiments on multiple real-world datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms several state-of-the-art baselines significantly."
